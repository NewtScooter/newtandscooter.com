Privacy policy.

Last updated November 12, 2026.

This notice describes the current Newt & Scooter Studios website. It may change as the site gains new features.

Information you provide

This site currently has no contact form, account sign-in, newsletter signup, or comment feature. It does not ask you to submit personal information.

Technical information

The hosting platform and its infrastructure providers may process technical request information to deliver and protect the site. This can include an IP address, browser or device details, the page requested, and the time of the request. Their handling and retention of that information are governed by their own terms and privacy practices.

Fonts and other services

This site loads fonts through Google Fonts. Your browser connects to Google to retrieve those font files, and Google may receive technical information about that request. See Google’s Privacy Policy for details.

Sharing

The share button uses your browser or device’s share feature when available. Otherwise, it copies the site address to your clipboard. Newt & Scooter Studios does not receive the destination or message you choose in your device’s share feature.

Analytics and advertising

This website currently does not use advertising or analytics tools.

External links

Links to other websites are governed by those sites’ own privacy practices. Social account links will be added here when the studio accounts are ready.

Privacy questions

Newt & Scooter Studios is based in Ontario, Canada. A public email address is not listed yet; when one is ready, it will appear on the Contact page. There is no direct-message form on this website at present.

Changes

If the site adds a form, mailing list, analytics, or another feature that changes how information is handled, this policy will be updated to describe it.
