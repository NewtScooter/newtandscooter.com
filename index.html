02 / First game

Wodipop

In development

Meet Wodipop! Wodipop is an original mobile app in development, created by a mom of two and designed to spark curiosity, creativity, and laughter through playful, hands-on discovery. Meet Wod, Odi, Dip, Ipo, and Pop! Five colorful little characters with big personalities, quirky abilities, and wonderfully unexpected ways of moving. Each brings something special to a world full of surprises, giggles, and delightful discoveries. With engaging game modes, intuitive touch-screen interactions, and open-ended play, Wodipop invites little explorers to tap, swipe, experiment, and discover at their own pace. And there are a few thoughtful extras for parents, too!

Wod, Odi, Dip, Ipo, and Pop, each a colorful new friend to discover and evolve with, children will love Wodipop's engaging game modes and enriching touch-screen interactions. Wodipop's unique personalities, body shapes, abilities, and movements bring surprises and giggles at every turn!
